About Us

Grazioso Salvare is an international organization dedicated to rescuing and training dogs for search-and-rescue missions, including water rescue, disaster response, and mountain operations. The organization partners with animal shelters in Austin, Texas, to identify dogs with the potential to serve in life-saving roles.

This platform has been modernized as part of a transition from a Python-based implementation to a full MEAN stack application (MongoDB, Express, Angular, and Node.js). The new architecture provides a scalable, modular, and responsive web-based system that improves performance, maintainability, and user experience.

Through an interactive dashboard interface, Grazioso Salvare staff can efficiently access and filter data from the Austin Animal Center (AAC) MongoDB database. The system supports full CRUD functionality - allowing users to create, read, update, and delete records - while enabling visual exploration of shelter data to identify dogs suitable for training programs.
