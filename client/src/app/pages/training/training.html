TRAINING PROGRAMS

Grazioso Salvare’s mission is to rescue and train dogs for search-and-rescue operations in water, disaster, and mountain environments.

Water Rescue Training

Dogs trained for aquatic environments such as floods, lakes, and coastal rescues.

  • Traits: Strong swimming ability, calm behavior
  • Breeds: Labrador Retriever, Newfoundland
  • Focus: Water entry, victim recovery

Disaster Rescue Training

Urban search-and-rescue in collapsed structures.

  • Traits: High focus, stability under stress
  • Breeds: German Shepherd, Malinois
  • Focus: Rubble search, scent detection

Mountain Rescue Training

Rugged terrain search and rescue operations.

  • Traits: Endurance, agility
  • Breeds: Husky, Border Collie
  • Focus: Tracking, navigation
